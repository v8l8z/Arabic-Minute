Overview

Arabic Minute ("the App") is an Arabic vocabulary learning application developed by Abdulla Alhemeiri. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App does and does not collect.

Arabic Minute works entirely offline, collects no personal data, and requires no account or login.

Data Collection

Arabic Minute does not collect, transmit, share, or store any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected
  • No usage analytics or tracking is used
  • No advertising frameworks or ad identifiers are used
  • No data is sent to any server
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data are included
  • No account, registration, or login is required

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party:

  • Your selected difficulty level
  • Words you have marked as favorites or learned
  • Your daily learning streak and XP progress
  • Quiz scores and game progress
  • Notification time preferences
  • App settings and preferences

This data is stored using standard on-device storage (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time through the App's Settings page or by deleting the App from your device.

Internet Access

Arabic Minute does not require an internet connection to function. All vocabulary data (1,200+ words), quizzes, grammar lessons, and features are bundled within the App and available offline.

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine for pronunciation. This is processed entirely on your device and does not require internet access.

Third-Party Services

Arabic Minute does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. No third-party SDKs that collect user data are included in the App.

Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it is safe for use by children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by:

  • Using the reset options in the App's Settings page
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes all associated data

There is no data stored on our servers to delete, as we do not collect or store any data.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date. As the App does not collect user data, we have no way to notify users of changes directly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
