Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

Overview

Arabic Minute is an Arabic vocabulary learning app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Data Collection

Arabic Minute does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your selected difficulty level
  • Words you have marked as favorites or learned
  • Your daily learning streak
  • Notification time preferences
  • Wallpaper customization preferences

Third-Party Services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts for display purposes. Google's privacy policy applies to that service. No other third-party services are used.

Analytics

Arabic Minute does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at abdulla.saeed.alhemeiri@gmail.com.
