Overview

Arabic Minute is an Arabic vocabulary learning app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device using localStorage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your selected difficulty level
  • Words you have marked as favorites or learned
  • Your daily learning streak and XP progress
  • Quiz and game high scores
  • Notification time preferences

Online Features

Most of Arabic Minute works entirely offline. However, the following optional features require an internet connection and send data to our server:

  • AI Conversation Practice: When you use the conversation feature, the text you type or speak is sent to our server to generate Arabic responses, translations, and grammar corrections. We do not store conversation data beyond what is needed to generate a response.
  • Text-to-Speech: For words without bundled audio, the app may send text to our server to generate pronunciation audio. If the server is unavailable, the app falls back to your device's built-in speech synthesis.
  • Speech Recognition: When you use the microphone in conversation practice, your voice is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition. The recognized text is then sent to our server as described above.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Fonts: The app loads fonts from Google Fonts for display purposes. Google's privacy policy applies to that service.

No other third-party services, analytics, or advertising frameworks are used.

Analytics

Arabic Minute does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The AI conversation feature processes text in real-time and does not store it.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date.
