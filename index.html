About

Arabic Minute helps you build Arabic vocabulary through daily practice. The app features daily word cards with transliteration and pronunciation, vocabulary quizzes, root exploration, grammar lessons, dialect comparison, calligraphy wallpapers, and more. The entire app works fully offline with no account required.

Features

  • Daily Arabic word with transliteration and meaning
  • Text-to-speech pronunciation
  • Vocabulary quizzes with multiple modes
  • Arabic root explorer
  • Grammar lessons and verb conjugation
  • Dialect explorer (MSA, Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf, Maghrebi)
  • Arabic letter guide
  • Sentence builder exercises
  • Listening practice
  • Calligraphy wallpaper generator
  • Daily challenges and achievements
  • Favorites and progress tracking
  • Works entirely offline

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Arabic Minute works entirely offline. All vocabulary, grammar, and lesson data is bundled with the app.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to the Settings page within the app. You can reset your streak, learned words, and favorites from there.

What Arabic levels are supported?

The app includes vocabulary across beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels covering everyday conversation, travel, and academic Arabic.

How does the daily word feature work?

Each day a new Arabic word is presented with its transliteration, English meaning, and example usage. Your progress is tracked with a daily streak counter.
