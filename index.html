About the App

Arabic Minute is a focused, distraction-free Arabic vocabulary learning app designed for daily practice. Each day you receive a new Arabic word complete with transliteration, English meaning, and contextual usage. The app includes 1,200+ carefully curated vocabulary words organized across four difficulty levels, from absolute beginner to advanced.

The entire app works offline with no account or internet connection required. All vocabulary data, quizzes, and features are bundled directly within the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Arabic Minute works entirely offline. All 1,200+ vocabulary words, grammar lessons, and quiz data are bundled within the app. You can learn anywhere without Wi-Fi or cellular data.

Does the app collect any personal data?

No. Arabic Minute does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All your progress, favorites, and settings are stored locally on your device only. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app requires no sign-up, login, or account of any kind. Simply download and start learning.

What Arabic levels are supported?

The app includes four difficulty levels: Beginner, Elementary, Intermediate, and Advanced. Words are drawn from everyday conversation, travel, academic, and professional contexts.

How does the daily word feature work?

Each day a new Arabic word is presented with its Arabic script, transliteration, English meaning, and usage context. Your progress is tracked with a daily streak counter and XP system to keep you motivated.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to the Settings page within the app. You can reset your learning streak, learned words, quiz scores, and favorites from there.

How does pronunciation work?

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce Arabic words. No internet connection is required for pronunciation.

Is the app available in languages other than English?

Currently, the app interface and word translations are in English. The vocabulary content is in Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), with dialect comparisons available in the Dialect Explorer feature.
